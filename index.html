Project Title: Storefront Website

NurturePath Lactation Services

NurturePath Lactation Services is a hybrid e-commerce and booking platform dedicated to providing expert, BSN-RN led breastfeeding support and curated nursing supplies.

The business caters to both first-time mothers navigating their initial breastfeeding journey and established mothers seeking solutions to specific lactation challenges. Customers visiting the site will be able to read educational resources, book in-person or telehealth consultations with a registered nurse, and purchase highly recommended breastfeeding accessories (such as pumps, soothing balms, and nursing pillows). The website will be built using standard front-end web technologies including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How the Online Business Works

The proposal describes a direct-to-consumer service and retail hybrid where customers can browse and book time slots for professional lactation consultations, add physical nursing products to a virtual shopping cart, and complete transactions through a single secure payment gateway.

1. Browse

Review consultations, services, and nursing supplies.

2. Select

Choose a service time or add a physical item to the cart.

3. Checkout

Enter payment, shipping, and appointment intake information.

4. Confirmation

Receive an order or booking confirmation after checkout.
